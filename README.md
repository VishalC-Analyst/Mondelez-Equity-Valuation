# Mondelez-Equity-Valuation
Equity research and valuation model for Mondelez International.

Equity research report and discounted cash flow (DCF) valuation of Mondelez International, a global snack and confectionery company. Valuation date: 1 December 2023.

## Objective
Analyse Mondelez's financial performance (2019 to 2023), forecast its financials to 2027, and estimate the value per share using DCF and relative valuation.

## Approach
- Analysed 5 years of financials: revenue, cost of sales, gross profit, operating income, net income, EBIT, EBITDA and EPS
- Built a 3-statement forecast (income statement, balance sheet, cash flow) to December 2027
- Valued the company using a DCF: free cash flow to firm, discounted at 5.16%, with terminal value by the Gordon Growth method (2.3% long-term growth) and by an exit multiple (13.2x EV/EBITDA)
- Ran sensitivity tables: WACC vs long-term growth rate, and WACC vs exit multiple
- Benchmarked against peers (Kellogg, Hershey, Campbell Soup, General Mills) on EV/Sales, EV/EBITDA and EV/EBIT, and calculated beta from weekly data

## Findings
- Revenue and profitability grew steadily from 2019 to 2023, with a dip in net income in 2022 linked to external factors such as the Russia-Ukraine war
- Mondelez holds the #1 global position in biscuits and #2 in chocolate
- The DCF value is highly sensitive to the discount rate and long-term growth assumptions, as the sensitivity tables show
